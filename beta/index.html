Know what your apps do. Stop them when they shouldn’t.

Pavu watches the network activity of the apps on your Mac and changes to your system, alerts you when something looks risky, and lets you cut a program off the network. Everything is analyzed on your Mac.

Download for macOS What Pavu does and doesn’t see

What it does

See every connection

Which app connects where, the destination’s country, city and network provider, and how much data it sends.

Spot risky behavior

Built-in rules flag unsigned apps or apps run from Downloads going online, large uploads, regular call-backs, unusual ports, inbound connections from the internet, suspicious launch items and listeners, apps posing as system programs, and turned-off system protections.

Get told right away

New findings arrive as macOS notifications. Each one explains why it was flagged and shows the evidence.

Decide what happens

Block a program’s new connections, or let a rule block automatically; programs that come with macOS are never blocked. Mark expected behavior as trusted. Every decision can be undone.

A finding is a reason to look closer, not proof of harm. If Pavu lacks the data a rule needs, it says so instead of reporting “safe”.

Privacy

Pavu looks at

  • Which program connects to which address and port, when, and how much data
  • Launch items, listening ports, system extensions and security settings

Pavu never collects

  • What you send or receive: messages, pages or files
  • Passwords, cookies or sign-in tokens
  • Decrypted traffic: it never intercepts encryption
  • Nothing is uploaded. Activity history stays on your Mac for 24 hours by default; you can pause monitoring or clear it at any time.
  • Locations come from a database built into the app, so IP addresses are never sent anywhere. Pavu only downloads its monthly update, which you can switch to manual.
